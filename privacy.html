Privacy Policy

MyEiruv ("we", "us") provides eruv boundary information and status alerts. This page explains what information we collect and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Account information: name and email address when you create an account.
  • Alert preferences: which eruv(s) you follow and how you'd like to be notified (email or SMS), if you set up alerts.
  • Location data: if you use the "Check my location" or address search feature, your coordinates are sent to our mapping provider (Mapbox) to determine your position — this is not stored by us beyond the current session.

How we use it

  • To show you which eruv(s) you're in and their current status.
  • To send status alerts you've opted into (via email or SMS).
  • To let contractors manage the eruv zones they're responsible for.

Third-party services

We use the following services to operate MyEiruv. Each has its own privacy policy governing data they process on our behalf:

  • Google Firebase — account authentication and data storage
  • Mapbox — maps and address search
  • Brevo — sending email and SMS alerts
  • Cloudflare — website hosting and security

Data sharing

We do not sell your information. We do not share it with third parties except the service providers above, solely to operate the features you use.

Your choices

You can update or delete your alert preferences at any time from your account. To delete your account entirely, contact us using the information below.
